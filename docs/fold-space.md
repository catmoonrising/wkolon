# Fold Space

Define this contract before calculations. Preserve Fold Space20418 and Force
Powers18202 verbatim with attribution. Full-Round Action. One held or unattended
object within6squares and Line of Sight, or one Vehicle occupied by the caster.
Everything inside the object moves with it. The power does not independently
target a creature or require a Defense check. UTF20/25/30/35/40 permits a
Medium/Large/Huge/Gargantuan/Colossal or smaller object to move up to
6/12/18/24/30squares respectively. Below20 has no effect. Use the selected
achieved tier for both limits; choosing a lesser tier does not retain either
limit of a higher tier.

The special FP has two mutually exclusive choices: increase maximum size by
two categories, capped at Colossal (Cruiser), OR double transport distance.
The size order above Colossal is Colossal (Frigate), then Colossal (Cruiser).
Do not apply both improvements for one FP or allow a second FP in the round.
Represent named enhancement choices in the shared power rule and shared saved
check finalization/UI. Resolve an explicit named choice after the check without
rerolling it. The ordinary check FP can unlock tiers but does not buy either
special improvement. Reject missing/unknown enhancement choices before any
mutation or randomness. Persist the selected enhancement only on a completed
reviewed activation, not a queued draft or a different power. Old manual
pending powers remain manual and cannot acquire retroactive transport results.

Reuse earned-tier choice, suite copies, one-FP ledger, Adept, Mastery,
natural20 recovery and action budgets. Previews/queueing do not roll or spend.
Mastery can combine with the special FP; Adept cannot. Natural20 does not
bypass a DC or double size/distance. No HP damage, teleport attack or critical
multiplier. Return structured maximumSize, distance, contentsTransported and
target scope plus a folded result. No map, NPC, inventory quantity or vehicle
location is silently changed. Target eligibility, actual size, range, Line of
Sight, path/destination placement and moving contents remain external, so
Partial. Improved Fold Space remains separately Manual until its FP/Destiny
alternatives are reviewed and implemented; do not imply those are automated.

Test threshold equality, lower tiers, both exclusive choices through shared
controls, size cap, ordinary FP, genuine Adept/Mastery/recovery, saved checks,
legacy protection, invalid-choice atomicity and old power isolation. Independent
review, combined checks, actual controls/mobile/refused offline and hosted
verification precede publication.
