# Drain Energy

Define this contract before calculations. Preserve Drain Energy20396 and Force
Powers18202 verbatim with attribution. Standard Action, one powered weapon or
object within6squares and Line of Sight. UTF20/25/30/35 drains all Energy Cells
powering a Small/Medium/Large/Huge or smaller object. Below20 has no effect.
The drained object cannot be used until its Energy Cells are replaced.
Only the DC35 tier additionally drains a Power Generator supplying the object.
Do not let the name imply HP damage, a defense comparison, Ion damage, an attack
roll, power recovery or a reduction of anyone's Force Points.

The special FP raises the maximum object size by one category, capped at
Gargantuan. It does not improve the check, unlock another tier's generator
clause, turn a Large object into Gargantuan, or bypass range/Line of Sight.
An enhanced DC30 permits Huge objects with Energy Cells; it does not grant the
DC35 generator clause. Choosing a lesser achieved tier uses that tier's size
and generator permission before applying the one-category enhancement.

Extend the shared non-damage power effect family with a closed energy-drain
record and structured maximum size, drained supply and replacement requirement.
The tier chooser labels Size and the actual base size, not Damage or dice.
Generalize the existing earned-tier/check/FP/Adept/Mastery pipeline rather
than inventing a parallel action or resource ledger. Post-check enhancement,
ordinary FP tier improvement, saved check reuse, pure queue/preview, one FP per
round, suite copy spending and natural20 recovery retain shared semantics.
Natural20 does not bypass a missing DC; Mastery does not roll a d20. Old manual
pending activations must not gain retroactive effects after importing a save.

The sheet owns no external target inventory or map. It computes the maximum
size and exact supply outcome, but actual target size, cells versus generator,
eligibility, range/Line of Sight and applying the loss to another sheet remain
external, so Partial. Do not silently drain the caster's weapon, reset their
FP, or promise automated opposing equipment. Display the replacement and
generator clauses behind folded details and preserve them in saved result text.

Test every threshold/lesser tier, enhanced size cap, generator gate before/after
enhancement, no-damage/no-defense output, ordinary versus special FP, genuine
Adept/Mastery/recovery, saved result/resource isolation and legacy protection.
Independent review, combined checks, actual controls/mobile/refused offline
and hosted verification precede publication.
