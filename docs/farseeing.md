# Farseeing

Define this contract before calculations. Preserve pinned Farseeing 18168 and Force
Powers 18202 articles verbatim. Full-Round Action, one creature known or met before.
Inclusive UTF versus Will. A dead target has Will 30. Success senses alive/dead,
vague immediate surroundings/current activity/strong emotions. Failure gives
no information, including alive/dead, and forbids using this power against that
same target for 24 hours. The special FP requires success and gives a clear
mental image of surroundings plus creatures/objects within 6 squares of target.
No actual secret or target status is invented by the sheet.

Capture a closed canonical snapshot of target display name, optional declared
Will and a known-dead declaration before rolling. Known-dead sets effective
Will 30; unknown status does not imply the target is alive. Normalize names
for the local failure ledger with trim/lowercase only, retaining display text.
This is a user-supplied target identity, not proof that aliases are different
creatures. Knowledge of the target and actual identity/information are external.

When Will is declared, compute success/failure directly from the final check.
When it is unknown, require an explicit GM-confirmed Success or Failure before
completing the result. Do not silently count it as a hit or a miss. Extend the
shared waiting-result contract with an explicit outcome-choice flag, without
changing old result shapes. The ordinary FP may improve the saved check first;
then the GM outcome must be confirmed for that improved total. Unknown-Will
buttons are Success, Failure, or Success with the special FP if affordable.
Known failures cannot buy a special FP. Invalid/premature/missing confirmations
fail before mutation/RNG. Freeze the confirmation against the final check.
Unconfirmed outcomes must remain resolvable with 0 FP or after paid Adept.

Use the shared FP/copy/action/Adept/Mastery/natural 20 recovery pipeline. Special
enhancement does not also buy a check bonus. Do not consume another copy,
reroll UTF or let a second FP slip through during outcome confirmation. A
failure lockout is recorded only after the final check/decision, so a valid
ordinary FP can rescue a provisional miss without creating a false lockout.
Natural 20 alone does not bypass Will or disclose a dead target.

Save closed failed-target rows with normalized identity, display name and
14400 rounds remaining (24h at 10 rounds/minute). Block every copy against that
identity until it expires. Reuse the shared explicit game-time advancement,
including turns, 1 minute suite recovery, consciousness waits, 10 minute
recuperation and 8 hour rest. Explicit New day advances 24h and expires it;
An 8 hour rest subtracts 4800 rounds rather than clearing a 24 hour lockout.
End combat/New encounter/natural 20 recovery/merely editing resources do not.
Do not use wall-clock time. Failure rows are independent of owning copy and
action history. Permit bounded manual remaining-minute correction/removal,
as with other saved timers. 1000 rows is a data limit, not a RAW target limit. Preflight recording
capacity before RNG/costs for a new identity, so a failure decision cannot
softlock an already-spent activation.

Old manual activations never acquire retroactive information or cooldowns.
Failure result text must not disclose the known-dead flag or imply life/death.
Actual Will when undeclared, knowledge/identity, information/images/GM judgment
remain external, so Partial. Use folded details and escaped names. Verify
known/unknown/dead/equal cases, 0 FP/Adept outcomes, ordinary/special FP, every
time/reset path, repeated-copy rejection, pure previews, legacy/source loss,
atomicity, old-power compatibility and actual controls/mobile/offline before
independent approval, combined checks and hosted publication.
