# Movement, Scent and Charge

This contract precedes integration calculations. Sources remain pinned and
unchanged: Veteran Talent Tree revision 20988 (Reckless, Galaxy at War),
Fleet-Footed revision 19662 (Scum and Villainy), Increased Agility revision
19177 (Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide), and Keen Scent revision
20686 (Rebellion Era Campaign Guide). The Fleet-Footed producer handoff's
Galaxy at War attribution was incorrect; the pinned article says Scum and
Villainy.

Reckless adds the Wisdom bonus, minimum +1, to damage in the shared Charge
plan. It requires Tested in Battle, preserves ordinary attack rows, combines
with Powerful Charge, and enters normal critical multiplication. A failed
Charge path never deals damage. It has no additional activation cost.

Fleet-Footed adds +2 to the Speed used to validate a Running Attack only when
the plan includes movement both before and after the attack. It preserves
terrain costs, movement modes, load restrictions and attack preflight. The
printed benefit to subsequent movement until the end of the turn remains
manual in this batch; this does not change the ordinary Speed statistic.

Increased Agility increases an existing Swim or Climb Speed by 2 before shared
movement penalties. It does not grant a missing movement mode or increase land
or Fly Speed. Conditioning and its Strength/Constitution prerequisites stay
enforced. The printed Jump-distance and climbing Dexterity clauses remain
manual pending their application contexts.

Keen Scent raises an Ewok's existing Scent range to 20 squares. It neither grants
Scent to other species nor broadens separately worded skill-reroll permissions.
Derived data, the Senses module and the stat block use the increased range. Detection of other
actors remains manual. All four records retain Partial status.
