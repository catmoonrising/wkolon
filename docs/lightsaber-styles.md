# Lightsaber styles and Resilience contract

This contract precedes calculations. Lightsaber Forms Talent Tree revision24952
supplies Shii-Cho and Makashi. Jedi Guardian Talent Tree revision26764 supplies
Resilience. Existing Lightsaber Defense, Jar'Kai, Block, Deflect, condition and
bonus-type contracts continue to apply.

Shii-Cho changes the shared Block/Deflect penalty per previous attempt from -5
to -2. It does not reset the attempt counter. Only chronologically valid active
ownership enables the style. A saved pending check retains its original bonus;
acquiring or losing the style cannot alter or reroll that check.

Makashi increases the deflection bonus gained from Lightsaber Defense by 2, to a
maximum of +5, while wielding one activated Lightsaber in one hand. Use current
weapon size and grip declarations. A quantity stack remains one wielded entry;
a single Double-Bladed weapon is not automatically two wielded Lightsabers.
Do not invent a ban on another type of weapon in the off hand. Switching grip,
ignition or wielded Lightsabers reevaluates this passive increase, without
spending another Swift action or changing the saved base activation. Acquiring
the talent can enable its passive increase; losing prerequisites suppresses it.
An existing larger bonus is not reduced by an increase capped at +5. Jar'Kai's
separate activation snapshot and weapon-change ambiguity remain unchanged.
Makashi contributes through the same typed deflection bonus, not a second stacking
bonus. It grants nothing without a Lightsaber Defense activation or while that
activation is suppressed by flat-footedness or helplessness.

Resilience moves up two Condition Track steps with a Full-Round action. It costs
no FP or daily use and does not heal HP. It cannot act while helpless, removes
no persistent cause, and clamps at Normal State. Its specific special recovery
permission uses the same distinction from ordinary Recover as Indomitable.
Repeated uses require the normal Full-Round budget. Queued previews mutate only
a copy, and incomplete/stale/invalid actions do not spend resources.

Keep source articles verbatim. Mechanical records remain Partial where external
context or hand declarations are still manual. Add no new activation controls
for the passive styles; existing reaction previews and Lightsaber Defense totals
must show their actual results.
