# Force Thrust

Define this contract before calculations. Preserve Force Thrust revision 24977,
Force Powers 18202 and the existing Moving a Door Jedi Counseling entry.
Reuse the normal power-copy, action, check, after-check FP, Adept, Mastery,
natural-20 suite recovery and transactional saved-result machinery. Keep the
catalog Partial: target checks, positions, collision geometry and HP belong to
an external character or encounter, not the user's editable character.

A Standard Action targets one object or character within 12 squares and Line
of Sight. Declare the opponent's rolled Strength check (including Strength),
Base Attack Bonus, size and exceptional stability separately. Add BAB, printed
size adjustment (Fine -20 through Colossal +20 in increments of 5), and +5
when more than two legs or otherwise exceptionally stable. Do not roll the
opponent's check on the user's behalf or add their Strength twice.

The final Use the Force check must strictly beat the opponent's modified check.
A tie does not push. Success pushes 1 + floor(margin / 5) squares away. Neither
a natural 1 nor 20 overrides the comparison, and no weapon attack critical,
minimum attack total or automatic success rule applies. Honor the target's
existing stability declaration, without inventing general immovability rules.

An optional special FP reduces the target's Strength check by 5. If the target
is pushed into a larger object it takes 1d6 damage, or 3d6 with the special FP.
Declare the distance to a larger blocking object, if any. Roll collision damage
only if the successful push reaches that object, after the final FP choice.
A check FP boosts the user's check; the special FP modifies the opponent and
collision dice. Both obey the shared ordinary one-FP-per-round ledger and must
not combine with paid Adept. Never apply collision damage to the user's HP or
invent target DR/SR/threshold effects. Display raw damage and pushed squares.

When jc:111-moving-a-door is enabled (the existing default), permit a Door
mode with a declared Break DC. The final Use the Force check meeting that DC
opens the door; otherwise it remains intact. Do not invent collision HP damage
or reduce its Break DC with the special FP's Strength-check penalty, since
this Counseling procedure replaces that check. Ordinary check FP remains
available. With Counseling disabled, offer only the normal RAW opposed-check
procedure. Preserve pinned Counseling text and the explicit off setting.

Queue and preview spend nothing. Save the original target declaration and
check; resolve after the final check/FP choice exactly once. Reloading must not
reroll or spend again. Validate closed data, source pins, opponent declarations,
checks and saved executable results. Older manual Force Thrust results remain
manual and never acquire a retroactive push or collision roll.

Use the pointer Actions form and existing Force Powers controls. Fold detailed
opponent arithmetic and raw collision rolls. Keep concise labels, default
folding, theme colors, accessible names, mobile overflow and real offline
loading. Before release, verify complete legal Adept/Mastery fixtures, ties,
five-point margins, all sizes, stability, both FP uses, collision reach/no-reach,
Counseling on/off, queue/save/reload/legacy and actual browser controls. Obtain
independent review, combined regressions and exact hosted-build verification.
