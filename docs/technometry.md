# Technometry

Define this contract before calculations. Preserve Technometry20066 and Force Powers18202
pinned articles verbatim. Standard Action, one Droid or electronic device
touched. Inclusive UTF versus Will. A successful information use yields one
piece of information plus one for every5points by which the check exceeds Will,
using Access Information from Use Computer for what information is available.
No actual secret is fabricated by the sheet. Below Will yields no information.
No minimum DC, damage, critical or natural20 automatic success is invented.

Alternatively, on success against a Droid choose exactly one: jam its senses
to permit Stealth against it even though aware, until the end of the caster's
next turn; deny Dexterity to Reflex against the caster's next attack made
before that deadline; or make it do nothing except flee from you at top speed on its
next2turns. These are alternatives to information, not cumulative effects.
They are not available against an electronic device that is not a Droid.

Special FP adds2pieces of information OR extends the selected Droid effect by
one additional round. It neither increases the check nor makes a failed check
succeed. Flee becomes3target turns; the other effects' deadline extends one
round beyond the end of the caster's next turn. It does not add extra attacks
to the denied-Dexterity option. Use the selected effect's enhancement, with no
extra generic named-choice prompt when the mode already determines it.

Capture a closed canonical snapshot of powerID, target kind (Droid/device),
effect and optional declared Will before rolling. Unknown Will returns a
conditional success boundary and information formula; known Will computes an
exact count/outcome. Freeze the snapshot across queue execution, saved check,
ordinary FP and special enhancement decisions. Queued raw selections remain
editable until execution. Malformed or incompatible choices fail before any
mutation/RNG. Known misses do not offer a useless special enhancement. Unknown
Will retains a conditional enhancement without pretending the target was hit.

Reuse the existing saved check/FP/Adept/Mastery, suite copy, recovery and
action-budget paths. An ordinary FP changes the check, not the special effect;
it may change success or information count. Old manual activations remain
manual without retroactive effects. Return structured conditional/exact
information count or the selected effect and deadline, with folded details.
No target's inventory, memories, defenses, movement, actions or knowledge is
silently changed, and the caster gains no unrelated Stealth or attack bonus.
Touch/eligibility, actual information, applying target effects and map/turn
ownership remain external, so Partial. Target memory access is not slicing,
a new Use Computer check or an attitude change.

Verify all5point boundaries/inclusive Will,4modes, device restriction, special
versus ordinary FP, frozen selection, invalid atomicity, genuine features,
legacy isolation, actual controls/mobile/refused offline and hosted gates.
Independent review and combined regression precede publication.
