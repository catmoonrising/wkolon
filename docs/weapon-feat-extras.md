# Additional weapon feat clauses

This contract precedes calculations. Keep the pinned Galaxy at War articles
Riflemaster20935 and Sport Hunter20955 unchanged. Extend the closed exact-weapon
`weaponTrait` effect with one field per record: `size: 'medium'` or
`pointBlankDice: 1`. Existing actual-weapon proficiency gating remains required.

Riflemaster treats a Light Repeating Blaster as Medium instead of Large. Apply
this to its derived weapon before the shared size/grip/full-attack consumers,
without changing catalog size, damage, weight, actual grip declaration or stock
rules. Do not imply a new general weapon-wielding restriction is automated.

Sport Hunter grants a Slugthrower Pistol one extra weapon damage die at Point-Blank
Range, using the shared actual-distance range band. Apply it only at band0 and
only to that equipment ID. This die is independent of Rapid Shot, Burst Fire and
Deadeye: their printed exclusions name each other, not Sport Hunter. Preserve
the existing maximum among those mutually excluded contributions, then add this
independent die. Keep it in un-Aimed variants and subsequent routine attacks;
all existing ammo, critical and hit/miss resolution stays shared.

Both feats remain Partial. Riflemaster's Carbine Bracing and Sport Hunter's
Sporting Blaster Pistol rerolls remain manual. No new character fields, actions,
resource spending, rules summaries or prerequisite exceptions are introduced.

Point-Blank benefits need a declared distance. An unknown range grants no
conditional die, matching the existing Point-Blank Shot behavior.
