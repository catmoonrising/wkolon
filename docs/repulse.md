# Repulse

Define this contract before calculations. Preserve pinned Repulse19561 and Force
Powers18202 articles verbatim. Standard Action, all adjacent targets. Roll one UTF
check and compare that same final result to each target's opposing Strength
check plus its Base Attack Bonus, size adjustment and stability. Use the shared
Force Thrust displacement arithmetic: strict success, tie fails; push1square
plus1per5points of margin. Size Fine/Diminutive/Tiny/Small/Medium/Large/Huge/
Gargantuan/Colossal adds-20/-15/-10/-5/0/+5/+10/+15/+20. More than two legs or
exceptional stability adds5. The declared Strength check already includes the
target's Strength modifier. Do not add it again or roll NPC checks.

Special FP applies-5to each opposing check and adds2d6to collision damage.
A successfully pushed target takes1d6 only if its declared push reaches a
larger object, or3d6 with the special FP. Roll separately for each collision,
after the final UTF/FP choice, once. No collision roll on a tie, failure, empty
declaration or unreachable object. No minimum DC, natural20 auto-success,
weapon critical, Evasion/cover/half-miss damage or Force damage type. This is
not Force Thrust's Door Counseling or Extended Force Thrust range procedure.

Accept a bounded list of declared adjacent targets, with optional display name,
opposing Strength check, BAB, size, stability and optional larger-object distance.
Freeze a closed canonical snapshot before the saved check.
Associate results by target index, not display name; duplicate names are legal. Keep raw queued
targets editable before execution; later raw edits must not change finalization.
Zero declarations is permitted, with no per-target result or invented target.
The list limit100is a saved-data bound, not a rule limiting affected targets.
Actual adjacency and completeness of the target list remain external.

Reuse the ordinary check FP versus special enhancement, one-FP ledger, Adept,
Mastery, suite copy and natural20 recovery paths. Queue/previews spend nothing
and roll nothing. Persist closed per-target collision results when the reviewed
activation completes, tied to its snapshot, final check and enhancement. This
allows every target's outcome to remain readable after reload without another
roll, including lists longer than the generic action text limit. Reconstruct
the saved per-target table deterministically from those records, not RNG.
Keep the generic saved action text bounded with a compact aggregate summary.
Reject malformed/inconsistent saved results and premature/repeated resolution.
Historical manual activations never gain retroactive pushes or damage.

Shared Force Thrust results, target snapshots, Door Counseling and old saved
characters must remain identical. Repulse has no Door mode/range technique and
must not mutate the caster's HP, inventory, position or targets' external state.
Object size/geometry, actual movement, collisions and target DR/SR/HP remain
external, so Partial. Show concise per-target controls, folded arithmetic and
saved results, escaped names, theme/mobile-safe tables and actual offline use.

Verify every size, ties/five-point margins/stability, mixed collision reach,
separate rolls, both FP uses, original check/targets through reload, no RNG in
queue/preview/saved display, bounded full result persistence, genuine feature
builds, legacy/other-power isolation, independent review and full release gates.
