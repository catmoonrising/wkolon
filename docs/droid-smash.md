# Droid Smash

This contract precedes integration. Preserve the 4th-Degree Droid Talent Tree
revision 26315, Droid Smash (Force Unleashed Campaign Guide), actual degree
access and class talent slots.

When a positive Strength bonus applies to a manufactured melee weapon actually
wielded in one hand, use twice that bonus for damage. Include light weapons;
exclude unarmed attacks, natural weapons, ranged/thrown attacks and two-handed
grips. Require the shared grip resolver to recognize a one-handed grip. The
talent does not require proficiency. Zero bonuses and Strength penalties remain
unchanged. Ordinary two-handed Strength damage already has its own calculation
and receives no additional doubling from Droid Smash.

Use the current resolved weapon and Strength score. Preserve other damage terms,
critical routines, existing Dexterity-damage replacements, droid arm/system
requirements and save data. Complete fixtures retain their processor, arms and
actual 4th-degree/Independent Droid access. Mark Partial because external
positioning and unresolved equipment effects remain manual.

Heavy-Duty Actuators is excluded from this integration while its interaction
with two-handed Strength damage awaits the user's ruling. Its independent
completed handoff and source are retained.
