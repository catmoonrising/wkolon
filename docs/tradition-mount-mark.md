# Tradition, mount and marked-target bonuses

This contract precedes integration. Preserve Believer Disciple tree revision
16197, Luka Sene tree 19229, Assassin tree 15944, and Momentum Strike 18980.
Keep actual tradition membership, Force Sensitivity, Farseeing knowledge,
Assassin entry and Murderous Arts I prerequisites. Use complete character
fixtures rather than changing talent trees to authorize a selection.

Sith Reverence gives +1 morale on every attack while a declared ally of equal
or higher Dark Side Score is within 20 squares and line of sight. Use the
shared typed attack resolver, including area attacks. Quickseeing gives +2
insight against the declared target; the Free action, Use the Force check,
Farseeing copy consumption, living target and turn expiry remain declared.
Ally positions, scores and sight also remain declared.

Murderous Arts II adds a separate fixed 1d6 against a marked opponent on
non-area melee and ranged attacks. Preserve its d6 when a weapon uses d4, d8
or another die. Momentum Strike instead adds one current weapon die to melee
attacks when a declared Beast mount or Speeder Bike moved at least its Speed
this turn. Riding, movement and marking remain manual. Mixed fixed and current
weapon dice must coexist in ordinary, variant and critical routine expressions.

Reuse target flags, strict save validation, typed bonuses and current weapon
resolution. Owner-only controls remain available for clearing saved declarations
after source loss. All four records stay Partial. Preserve unchanged character
data, other target bonuses, sources and offline behavior.
