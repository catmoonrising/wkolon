# Fear and Obscure

Define this contract before calculations. Preserve Fear19129, Obscure20268,
Force Powers18202 and shared condition/action/FP/descriptor rules. Both remain
Partial because opponent Defenses, immunities, turns, actual actions and attacks
are external. Do not modify the caster's own actions, attacks or Defenses.

Fear is a Swift Action against one creature within12squares and Line of Sight.
A UTF check meeting target Will affects it at DC15/20/25/30: only one Standard,
Move, Swift or no Actions on its next turn. Use shared normal action meanings;
no extra NPC actions or out-of-turn prohibition is invented. This is a Fear
effect. Do NOT import Mind Trick's fleeing, wounded-end or lower-level target
rules: they are a different power/application. A successful special FP also
imposes -2 to all target Defenses until the beginning of the CASTER's next
turn. That penalty does not help this power hit its initial Will comparison.
The power has Dark Side and Mind-Affecting descriptors: shared DSS costs and
forbidden ordinary dark-side check-FP bonuses apply, including genuine Many
Shades descriptor removal, while Adept/special FP retain their permissions.

Obscure is a Reaction against one target within12squares and Line of Sight
that has just made an attack roll. Require a distinct declared trigger through
the shared Reaction ledger. UTF >= Will imposes -5 on that triggering attack.
If it misses, the caster may have the attacker reroll against ANOTHER creature
adjacent to the ATTACKER, also at -5. The optional reroll is not copied from
the original total, not another caster attack and not a second -5 on one roll.
Do not roll for the external attacker, invent a target or award a new action.
Special FP extends the penalty to all that target's attacks until the start of
the TARGET's next turn. Do not substitute caster-next-turn timing. The printed
power lacks Negate Energy's awareness/flat-footed and Energy restrictions;
retain common activation eligibility without adding those guards. Ordinary
Free-action FP check bonuses remain unavailable on a Reaction. Obscure is
Mind-Affecting, without an invented Dark Side descriptor.

Reuse shared action/copy costs, after-check special FP versus ordinary FP,
one-FP ledger, Adept/Mastery, natural20 recovery, save/replay and older manual
result handling. Fear's earned/lower tiers use Restriction labels, not damage
or higher allowed actions. Check-FP boosts after Many Shades removal can unlock
a second saved tier choice without spending/rerolling twice. Obscure's fixed
effect offers no fake damage tier. Unknown Will remains a conditional hit limit.

Keep conditional attack/Defense penalties and reroll/duration details folded.
Queue/preview cost nothing; finalize once. Cache assets and verify real complete
Adept/Mastery/Aing-Tii builds, all tiers and inclusive ties, descriptor behavior,
round ownership, triggering-event replay, both FP uses, target-state isolation,
