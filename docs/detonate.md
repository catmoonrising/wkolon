# Detonate

Define this contract before calculations. Preserve Detonate20139 and Force
Powers18202 verbatim with attribution. Standard Action, Telekinetic, one
unattended object within12squares and Line of Sight. UTF20/25/30/35 deals
4/6/8/10d6 damage to that object. Below20 has no effect. There is no printed
defense comparison, area attack, natural20 critical, Force damage descriptor,
damage-threshold change or attack-roll substitution. Do not invent one.

The special FP adds2d6 when activating the power. Its activation option is a
predeclared enhancement, not an after-check enhancement prompt. Deduct the FP
on actual activation before rolling, even if the check is below20; a queue,
preview or invalid action spends nothing and rolls nothing. This shares the
round FP ledger, so it cannot combine with Adept's cost or an ordinary check
FP. Without the special enhancement, an otherwise eligible ordinary FP may
improve the original saved check and unlock tiers, without adding2d6 damage.
Keep Mastery, Adept, suite-copy spending, natural20 recovery and lesser earned
tiers on the shared paths. Mastery may use the prepaid special enhancement
because Take10 itself costs no FP. Taking a lesser tier never refunds the FP.

Extend the closed outgoing-power family to express an unopposed damage result.
Output the rolled damage and its unspecified descriptor with an unattended
object scope. Do not fabricate a Reflex/Fortitude/Will interval or use the
word Force as a damage type. The shared result must still include the damage
roll and fold its detail by default. Roll damage once after final check/tier
choice; retain prepaid status in the activation snapshot across save/reload,
without deducting the special FP a second time or replaying old manual history.

Actual object eligibility, range/Line of Sight, DR, SR, HP, destruction and any
consequences of damaging an explosive object remain external, so Partial.
The name Detonate does not make the damage an explosion or an area attack.
Existing defended outgoing powers and saved snapshots remain unchanged.
