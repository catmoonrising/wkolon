# Rend

Define this contract before calculations. Preserve Rend revision20033 and Force
Powers revision18202, their attribution and verbatim articles. Rend is a
Standard Action, Dark Side power targeting one creature or object within
6squares and Line of Sight. An inclusive Use the Force versus Reflex check
deals3d6 damage on success and no damage on failure. Do not invent a minimum
check, area attack, half damage on a miss, size modifier or damage descriptor.

Rend explicitly doubles damage when its Use the Force check is a natural20.
This is a power-specific exception, not permission to critically hit with all
Force powers. A natural20 still compares the final check to Reflex; it does
not automatically succeed. Keep the selected natural die from the actual
activation, including Adept's kept die. Mastery's Take10 and ordinary FP added
to a check are not natural20. Double the single damage result, including the
special additional2d6, rather than making a second damage roll or doubling the
check. Preserve the general natural20 suite-recovery rule independently.

After a successful check, a special FP adds2d6 damage. Ordinary FP check
improvement and this enhancement remain mutually exclusive under the shared
one-FP-per-round ledger. The current outgoing resolver displays defense
intervals rather than owning a target. Before enabling this success-only
enhancement, require an explicit successful-hit declaration or a validated
target Reflex comparison. Do not infer a hit merely from a nonempty tier,
or silently spend this enhancement on a known miss. A queued plan or preview
must neither roll nor deduct resources. Finalization uses the saved check,
rolls damage once and survives reload without rerolling or paying twice.

If Rend's damage reduces a target to0HP, the target is ripped into two parts;
a living creature is automatically killed. Retain that outcome in structured
result metadata and folded text. Do not turn it into an unconditional death
on any hit, change damage-threshold steps, assume a droid is living, or mutate
the caster's HP/CT. Actual opponent HP, protection, living status, range and
Line of Sight remain external, so the feature remains Partial. State that
remaining boundary in the import review rather than promising automated NPCs.

Extend the closed outgoing-power schema and shared activation/check/FP path,
not a parallel attack engine. Keep older result/save shapes valid and protect
historical manual Rend activations from retroactive damage rolls. Source pins,
other outgoing-power damage and natural20 behavior must remain unchanged.
Test equality/miss, chosen natural20 versus modified20/Take10,3d6/5d6,
enhancement eligibility and ledgers, original-check reload, recovery, and
living0HP wording. Independent review, combined checks, actual controls,
mobile/offline and hosted verification precede publication.
