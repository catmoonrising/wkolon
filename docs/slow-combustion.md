# Slow and Combustion

Define this contract before calculations. Preserve Slow19342, Combustion20117,
Force Powers18202, Attacks27406 and the existing shared rules. Keep both
catalog records Partial: target Defenses, positions, immunity, DR/SR, HP,
conditions and subsequent turns are external to the user's sheet.

Slow is a Standard Action against one creature or Droid within12squares and
Line of Sight. UTF DC15/20/25/30 reduces Speed by1/2/3/4squares until the
beginning of the caster's next turn, only when the check equals or exceeds
the target's Fortitude. Large/Huge/Gargantuan/Colossal add5/10/20/50 to their
Fortitude against this power, smaller sizes0. Successful targets are also
Encumbered as a Heavy Load, with the printed -10 on Acrobatics, Climb,
Endurance, Initiative, Jump, Stealth and Swim. Do not invent a target Speed,
minimum Speed, additional damage or persistent caster debuff. One optional
special FP imposes -5 on target Fortitude against this use, raising the raw
Fortitude hit limit by5; it does not increase the chosen Speed reduction.

Combustion is an explicitly printed Area Attack, targeting every target in a
2x2square area entirely within12squares and Line of Sight. Compare the UTF
check inclusively against Fortitude. Roll4d6 raw Fire damage once after the
final FP choice. Reuse the existing Area Attack half-miss, Evasion and Cover
calculation without importing ordinary attack-roll natural1/20 critical or
minimum-total rules. On a hit, the target catches Fire; the ongoing Fire's
attack bonus is this final UTF check result, replacing its normal attack
bonus. Do not roll later Fire attacks or damage on the user's behalf.

Combustion's special FP moves ONE target hit by this power -1CT. It never
adds damage dice, moves every hit target, or applies the step on a miss. Keep
the chosen external target explicit in the result wording; the sheet cannot
identify tokens or mutate another character. Other threshold/Fire/immunity
rules and damage application remain external. No Dark Side descriptor is
invented for either power; use shared descriptor/DSS rules.

Reuse original action/check/suite costs, normal after-check FP bonuses versus
special enhancement, the ordinary one-FP ledger, Adept, Mastery and natural20
suite recovery. Queue/preview cost nothing. Save original checks and earned
lesser Slow tiers; if a check FP unlocks more tiers, retain a second effect
choice before finalization without paying/rerolling twice. Below Slow DC15
there is no effect. Saved executable result guards remain closed, and older
manual pending checks remain manual without retroactive effects.

Extend shared effect choices with a Speed label rather than pretending Slow
rolls damage. Fire result labels and structured data must identify Fire,
not Force damage; preserve all existing outgoing power formatting and math.
Keep ongoing Fire, Heavy Load and one-target CT detail folded by default.
Cache new assets and verify real complete Adept/Mastery builds, every tier,
size/FP thresholds, half-damage rounding, Evasion/Cover, hit-only Fire/CT,
saves/replay, ordinary descriptor behavior, actual controls, mobile and refused
network offline operation. Independent review, combined regressions and exact
hosted verification precede release.
