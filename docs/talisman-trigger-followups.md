# Talismans and triggered defenses

This contract precedes integration. Pinned sources remain unchanged: Dark Side
Devotee Talent Tree revision 18082, Jedi Guardian Talent Tree revision 26764,
Trickery Talent Tree revision 19801, and Jedi Sentinel Talent Tree revision
26765. Preserve their printed prerequisites and actual class access.

Dark Side Talisman grants +2 Force to one Defense against powers with the Light
Side descriptor. Greater grants it to all three. These do not apply to arbitrary
powers from a user with Dark Side Score 0. One `darkSideTalisman` inventory
designation (Reflex, Fortitude, Will, or `all`) is permitted, independently of
ordinary Force Talismans. The item must be carried and the appropriate talent
active. Conditional totals use shared highest-of-type Force stacking. Creating
an item, its Force Point/Full-Round cost, destruction and recreation clock remain
manual personal-item declarations, as with ordinary talismans.

Defensive Acuity uses the shared end-of-next-turn clock after the player
declares Fight Defensively. It adds one current damage die to melee Lightsaber
attacks and +2 circumstance to Use the Force checks for Block or Deflect.
Ordinary Use the Force and unrelated attacks remain unchanged. The check bonus
is included in the original check snapshot and retained on its rerolls. The
existing Fight Defensively control also sets this trigger; its existing stance
declaration does not separately deduct a Standard Action. Manual trigger
controls remain available for actions declared outside the sheet. Thrown
Lightsabers require their separate rules.

Improved Soft Cover becomes a Swift action through the shared action engine.
Activation requires its talent, a declared adjacent creature and available
actions, and grants +2 Cover Reflex until the start of the next turn. Queuing
does not spend or activate it; executing does. The existing Defense checkbox
uses the same activation and action spending. Clearing it represents losing
adjacency and never refunds the action. External positioning remains declared.

Gradual Resistance uses an encounter trigger after damage from a Force Power,
granting +2 Force to each Defense against that particular power. The player
identifies qualifying powers; the sheet never treats it as protection from all
powers. Conditional totals preserve ordinary Defenses and existing Force bonus
competition. Encounter reset clears the trigger. All five talents stay Partial.
