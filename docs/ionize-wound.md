# Ionize and Wound

Define this extension before calculations. Preserve Ionize revision 19182,
Wound 19375, Force Powers 18202, Attacks 27406 and Ion Damage 26732. Reuse
the reviewed outgoing power, skill check, action, suite-copy, FP and saved
choice resolvers. Keep both catalog records Partial.

Ionize takes a Standard action and targets one target within six squares
and line of sight. The check must meet DC 20/25/30 for 4d6/5d6/6d6 Ion
damage and equal or exceed Reflex. A miss or a check below DC 20 has no
effect. One optional FP adds 2d6 Ion damage. Permit lesser achieved tiers
before or after the check. Label the result as raw Ion damage, not HP lost:
target protection precedes HP halving and the penetrating pre-halving damage
is used for the target's Ion threshold comparison. Reuse the existing target
incoming-damage resolver when applying a result; do not halve the outgoing
roll or bypass the target's saved Ion ambiguities, cybernetics or protections.

Wound takes a Standard action and targets one creature within six squares
and line of sight. A check equal to or above Fortitude deals 4d6 Force
damage; a miss deals none. Printed Fortitude size modifiers are Large +5,
Huge +10, Gargantuan +20 and Colossal +50. An optional FP adds 2d6. If this
damage moves the target down the Condition Track, that condition is
persistent until DC 20 Treat Injury (Surgery). This is conditional on damage
causing a step; do not add an unconditional CT step. Preserve the treatment
requirement in structured data and the result. Do not infer an additional
breathing requirement from descriptive lung wording.

Both powers are single-target, without area Evasion/Cover or half misses.
Use derived descriptors for DSS and check-FP availability, including Many
Shades. Do not apply weapon natural-1/20 rules, criticals, a minimum attack
total or consume Battle Strike. Retain Adept, Mastery, natural-20 suite
recovery and the one-FP ledger. Queue is free; damage rolls once after the
final FP/effect choice. Preserve pending original checks/snapshots on reload,
atomic failure and older saved manual results without retroactive damage.

Actual targets, range/sight, immunities, HP/DR/SR, Ion adjustments and
applying/removing persistent conditions remain external to outgoing tables.
Display this information with concise type labels and folded details. Verify
closed data/pins, complete legal acquisition, actual controls, resource and
