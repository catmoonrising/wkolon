# Force Lightning

Define this extension before calculations. Preserve Force Lightning revision
18188, Force Powers 18202 and Attacks 27406. Use the reviewed outgoing power
resolver, original Use the Force check, action, suite-copy and Force Point
ledger. Do not introduce weapon attack modifiers, automatic natural-1 misses,
natural-20 hits, critical damage or consumption of Battle Strike.

One Standard action targets one target within six squares and line of sight.
A check equal to or above Reflex deals 8d6 Force damage and moves the target
one step down the Condition Track. A miss deals half damage, rounded down,
without a Condition Track step. An optional Force Point adds one more step
on a successful hit, without increasing damage. There is no activation DC.

This is a single-target power. Half damage on a miss does not make it an area
attack or grant an Evasion or Cover damage column. Extend the shared outgoing
defense-range calculation to express non-area half misses explicitly, while
preserving ordinary weapons, Slam and Blast. Show the printed power's extra
Condition Track steps separately from damage; damage-threshold effects,
target DR/SR, defenses, positioning and actually editing targets remain
external. Keep the catalog entry Partial.

The existing derived descriptor controls Dark Side Score and eligibility for
a check-bonus Force Point. Using Dark Side Lightning increases DSS once and
forbids a check-bonus FP; its printed enhancement remains available. A legal
Many Shades of the Force choice removes the descriptor, so these restrictions
do not apply. Preserve the existing rules for Adept, Take 10, recovery,
one FP per round and natural-20 suite recovery. Do not alter pinned articles.

Queue and preview do not spend or roll. Commit the original check, action,
suite copy and DSS atomically, and roll damage once after any pending FP
decision. An invalid roll leaves the character unchanged. Save/reload retains
the pending check and snapshot. Completed steps cannot roll again. Older
manual pending Lightning checks finish under their original manual behavior,
without fabricated damage or snapshots. Validate the exact closed data and
complete legal fixtures, actual controls, mobile rendering and offline use.
