# Force Blind

Define this contract before calculations. Use the pinned Force Blind article,
Force Powers and ordinary FP/action rules. The power is Swift, one creature
within12squares and Line of Sight. One UTF check meeting Reflex makes the
target Flat-Footed, and all OTHER creatures have Concealment from that target,
until the start of the CASTER's next turn. Targets which do not rely on
eyesight are immune. Unknown target Reflex remains a conditional hit limit.
Do not apply a generic Blind condition, Total Concealment, an arbitrary attack
penalty, global target flags or effects to the caster. Target Defenses,
senses, positions and attacks remain external; retain Partial.

An optional FP is committed before the activation/check to replace Swift
with Free, including failed checks. It is not a post-roll enhancement and
does not grant extra effects. Reuse one-FP-per-round and action allocation;
Adept and accelerated activation cannot both be bought with the same round's
FP. Mastery Take10 does not roll. Natural20 suite recovery remains shared.
Generalize the existing Energy Resistance quickening contract rather than
adding a second action-cost path. Preserve Energy Resistance's Swift cost,
FP charge, UI and saved histories. Do not offer a fake second enhancement.

The declared choice survives queue/save/reload; planning is free. Ordinary
FP check bonus stays subject to current-turn/shared FP restrictions. Commit
accelerated Free cost and FP exactly once, not on preview or effect completion.
Old manual results retain no retroactive target effects. Conditional target
instructions and immunity/duration are visible with folded detail. Cache docs,
preserve pins/text, test ordinary/accelerated actual controls, own-stat
isolation, failed-check FP cost, real Adept/Mastery, saved/replay and offline.
Independent review and complete combined/hosted gates precede publication.
