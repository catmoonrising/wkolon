Force Powers articles. Swift Action targets one adjacent target; compare UTF
inclusively against Fortitude for2d6 Force damage and a1square push away from
you. The push does not provoke Attacks of Opportunity. A miss deals no damage
and does not push. A UTF check is not a weapon attack roll: no critical,
natural1miss, natural20hit, or invented minimum total.

to each Fortitude. This does not add damage dice or make it an area attack;
no half-on-miss or area Evasion/Cover effects are invented. Keep the scope in
structured output and visible summary, with the printed push and unprovoking
movement in each hit row. Do not move the caster, mutate NPC positions/HP or
select actual enemies automatically. Remain Partial for external targets,
protection, immunity and board state.
