# Rulings register

These decisions are retained for later review. Ambiguities switches are saved
per character and change the indicated calculation or action resolution. A
default-only decision remains here even though it adds no switch to the sheet.

## Default-only choices

| Topic | Retained ruling | Alternative not added |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bracing stock requirement | The stock clause applies to Pistols. Other printed weapon/grip restrictions still apply. | Require an extended retractable stock for Heavy Weapons and Rifles as well. |
